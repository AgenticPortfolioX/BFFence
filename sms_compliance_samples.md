# SMS Compliance Samples: BF Fence

Use these samples for your A2P 10DLC registration. They have been updated to include mandatory branding, support, and opt-out instructions.

**Brand identified + Opt-out + Support**

> "BF Fence: Hi [Customer Name], thanks for requesting a fence estimate! We'll reach out shortly. Reply STOP to opt-out, HELP for help."

## Mandatory Requirements Checklist (Verified)
- [x] **Brand Identity:** Every message starts with "BF Fence".
- [x] **Opt-out:** Every message includes "Reply STOP to opt-out" or similar.
- [x] **First Touch:** Identifies brand and provides HELP/STOP instructions.
